# अक्षय ❤️ विभूति — Royal Jain Wedding Invitation

An exceptionally luxurious, royal Jain wedding invitation website crafted with **React.js, Tailwind CSS, TypeScript, and Framer Motion**.

Inspired by traditional Jain temple architecture, Rajputana palaces, antique gold craftsmanship, royal wedding mandaps, and premium handcrafted Indian wedding cards.

---

## 👑 Visual Identity & Theme
- **Theme**: Royal Jain Wedding — Traditional, Spiritual, Luxurious and Elegant.
- **Color Palette**:
  - Deep Royal Maroon: `#570D19`
  - Burgundy: `#380710`
  - Antique Gold: `#C6A15B`
  - Champagne Gold: `#E6D3A3`
  - Ivory: `#FFF8E9`
  - Warm Cream: `#F5EBD7`
- **Authentic Traditional Elements**:
  - Sacred Jain Prateek Chinha & Navkar / Mangalacharan motifs
  - Traditional Kalash with mango leaves and coconut
  - Multifoil palace arches and carved jali pillars
  - Continuous royal silk & velvet background texture with gold floral damask
  - Toran doorways with hanging brass bells and marigold garlands
  - Traditional Diyas with animated warm golden flames

## 🏛️ Features & Structure

1. **Grand Opening Screen (Royal Invitation Experience)**:
   - Full-screen deep maroon background with Rajasthani jharokha palace arch.
   - Sacred Jain Manglik symbol with golden radiance.
   - Sequential golden animation for "शुभ विवाह" and "अक्षय ❤️ विभूति".
   - "आमंत्रण देखें" (Open Invitation) button with golden flower petal shower.

2. **Jain Mangalacharan (जैन मंगलाचरण)**:
   - Sacred Jain temple inscription shloka:
     > *मंगलम् भगवान वीरो मंगलम् गौतमो गणः।*
     > *मंगलम् कुन्दकुन्दाद्यो जैन धर्मस्तु मंगलम् ॥*
   - Devotional invitation message and English translation.

3. **Royal Couple Introduction (वर एवं वधु)**:
   - Symmetrical ornamental royal frames with central golden gathbandhan divider.
   - **Groom**: अक्षय (Akshay) — सुपुत्र: श्री दिनेश जी एवं श्रीमती करुणा (निवासी: ऋषभदेव, उदयपुर, राजस्थान).
   - **Bride**: विभूति (Vibhuti) — सुपुत्री: श्री हेमंत जी एवं श्रीमती अर्चना (निवासी: फिरोजाबाद, उत्तर प्रदेश).

4. **Wedding Celebrations (शुभ कार्यक्रम)**:
   - **हल्दी समारोह**: मंगलवार, 24 नवंबर 2026 • प्रातः 10:00 बजे से
   - **मेहंदी समारोह**: मंगलवार, 24 नवंबर 2026 • सायं 4:00 बजे से
   - **शुभ विवाह**: बुधवार, 25 नवंबर 2026 • प्रातः 7:00 बजे से (Royal Mandap)
   - **Live Countdown Timer** to the auspicious Muhurta.
   - One-tap **"Add to Calendar"** integration for all ceremonies.

5. **Royal Wedding Venue (विवाह स्थल)**:
   - **Hotel Madurai**, 118 Hotel Street, Udaipole Road Behind HDFC Bank, Udaipole, Shrinath Market, Udaipur, Rajasthan 313001.
   - "विवाह स्थल देखें" (GET DIRECTIONS) button linked directly to Google Maps navigation.
   - Embedded interactive location map & quick copy address tool.

6. **Family Welcome (सादर स्वागत एवं अभिनंदन)**:
   - Hosts: **आस्था एवं निहाल जी**, **प्रणय 'जी' एवं अनंत**.

7. **Grand Closing & Royal Footer**:
   - Mandap illustration, heartfelt benediction: *"आपकी गरिमामयी उपस्थिति हमारे लिए सौभाग्य एवं हर्ष का विषय होगी।"*
   - Continuous seamless deep maroon & antique gold background from header to footer bottom.

8. **Royal Wedding Shehnai & Sitar Ambience**:
   - Pure harmonic Web Audio API Indian Classical Shehnai & Tanpura drone synthesizer (Raag Bilawal).
   - Floating music control with animated equalizer waves.

9. **Social & Sharing**:
   - Pre-formatted WhatsApp invitation share button.
   - Open Graph tags & SVG card preview for WhatsApp and Facebook.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack
- **Framework**: React 18 + TypeScript + Vite
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS with custom metallic gold gradients and antique foil effects
- **Animations**: Framer Motion
- **Icons**: Lucide React + Custom SVG Traditional Motifs
- **Celebration Effects**: Canvas Confetti (Royal Gold & Rose Petal Shower)
- **Audio**: Web Audio API Classical Shehnai Synthesis
